# ABC Communications — Customer Churn Analysis

**Data Analytics Internship Programme | AnalystLab Africa Consulting | Week 1**

## Project Overview
This project investigates customer churn for ABC Communications Ltd, a telecom
provider offering phone, DSL, and fibre-optic internet services. As a Junior
Data Analyst at AnalystLab Africa Consulting, I analysed 7,043 customer
records to identify churn drivers and produce actionable retention
recommendations.

**Dataset:** [Telco Customer Churn](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn) (Kaggle)

## Business Questions Answered
1. What does the customer base look like?
2. Which segments have the highest churn?
3. Does contract type influence retention?
4. Does tenure affect loyalty?
5. Which services influence churn?
6. Which payment methods have higher churn?
7. What actions should management take?

## Key Findings
- **Overall churn rate: 26.5%**
- **Contract type** is the strongest churn driver — month-to-month customers
  churn at 42.7% vs. just 2.8% for two-year contracts (a 15x gap)
- **Churn is front-loaded**: 47.4% in the first 12 months, dropping to 6.6%
  after 5 years
- **Fibre-optic** customers churn at 41.9%, more than double DSL customers
- **Electronic check** payers churn at 45.3%, ~3x automatic payment methods
